# Cognitive Focus Dialogue Rules (`theya.voice` / `they.audio`)

## 1. Core Product Mission
The two-person conversational mode is **NOT** a generic podcast generator for content creators. It is a **Cognitive Focus & Learning Instrument** designed for readers and listeners who struggle with attention drift, reading fatigue, or dense walls of text. Every turn of dialogue exists to keep the learner locked in and help them absorb complex ideas effortlessly.

## 2. Hard Prohibitions (Never Violate)

### Rule A: No Ping-Pong Verbatim Reading
- **Forbidden:** Speaker A reads Paragraph 1 verbatim, then Speaker B reads Paragraph 2 verbatim, alternating mechanically like two robots taking turns reading a PDF.
- **Required:** Speakers must hold distinct cognitive roles:
  - **Speaker A (`ANCHOR`):** Delivers the primary thesis, evidence, and narrative progression in clear, digestible chunks.
  - **Speaker B (`CLARIFIER` / Focus Partner):** Steps in at points of high cognitive density, transitions, contrasts, or revelations to ask the grounding question the learner is thinking, unpack jargon into plain language, or lock in the core takeaway.

### Rule B: No Cheesy AI Podcast Banter
- **Forbidden:** Artificial enthusiasm, hollow filler, and cliché podcast intros/outros such as:
  - *"Welcome back to the deep dive!"*
  - *"Buckle up!"*
  - *"Wow, that's so fascinating!"*
  - *"That's a great point!"*
  - *"Hey everyone, today we're unpacking..."*
- **Required:** Start immediately with the core tension or thesis of the material. Interjections must be substantive, direct, and focused on clarity (*"Hold on—what does that mean in practice?"*, *"Here is the part worth locking in:"*, *"Wait—why does that happen?"*).
