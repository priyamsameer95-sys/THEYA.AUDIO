# A second homepage, evaluated through the visitor's task

## Preserved comparison

Version A is the reviewed personal homepage at local commit `c9a5006`, branch `codex/homepage-before-taste-2026-10-15`. It includes the cream/ink/colour identity, original painting, separate work panels, CV and email copying. The new work is isolated on `codex/homepage-taste`. Neither branch has been pushed.

The user-supplied taste synthesis and dinner-splitting essay are design input, not new portfolio claims or instructions to publish the essay. Their useful standard here is: preserve the visitor's situation, distinguish necessary complexity from organizational baggage, make consequential choices inspectable, and state what could prove the design wrong.

## The real moment

A hiring leader opens the portfolio from a CV or introduction. They need to identify Priyam's current responsibility, select evidence relevant to their role, distinguish his decision from a team's outcome, and reach the CV or a contact channel. They do not need to work through the portfolio's entire company history or reconcile why AI explorations are in a separate late section.

This audience comes from the existing brief. The assumption that lending/AI work deserves the first path is an implementation judgment, based on Priyam's current remit and the earlier selected-work priorities. It is not a universal ranking of his career.

## Alternatives considered

1. Preserve the current order and improve the wording of five independent panels. This keeps breadth visible early, but leaves a lending reader to traverse unrelated sectors before the prototype and policy proposal.
2. Keep the personal opening, then connect the delivered lending journey to the prototype and policy proposal; offer ordinary anchor links to lending, agriculture and founder work. This serves the current remit while allowing other hiring readers to skip directly to their context.

Choose the second. No tabs, questionnaires or hidden panels. Keep all five previews visible and each status explicit. Retain the standalone Absolute and UrbanKhet boundaries, actual media and micro-interactions. Do not replace the painting, add a new visual theme, publish an AI capability or invent measured results.

## Changes and predictions

| Choice | Mechanism and intended consequence | What could change the judgment |
|---|---|---|
| State current level and supported P&L responsibility first | A first-time reader can place the remit without inferring seniority from project names. | Hiring readers still describe only a founder/artist, or the sentence obscures the introduction. |
| Name the consequential choice in each preview | The reader can choose a case by its problem and decision before opening another page. | Readers recall only the product name or image, or the preview overstates what was delivered. |
| Place EduLoans and MCP beside the lending story in the sequence | A lending/AI reader reaches uncertainty handling and policy control without traversing all other industries. | They mistake the prototype/proposal for production work, or non-lending readers struggle to reach their examples. |
| Use optional native context jumps | Different hiring contexts remain accessible without a role picker or a filter. | Readers interpret these as hidden tabs, find wrong targets or fail to retain their place. |
| Replace repeated career scope with a concise Experience bridge | The homepage gives evidence priority; the existing CV supplies chronology and responsibilities. | Readers cannot place the company relationships or find the complete record/CV. |

## Content contract

- College.money: connect case readiness to the lender handoff; Priyam owns journey, operations and commercials. Public reference captures do not establish historical authenticated completion, causal conversion or profitability.
- EduLoans: deterministic policy, visible missing thresholds and human verification; original prototype with sample records/scripted responses. Recorded release restrictions are not presented as resolved.
- MCP: separate evaluation, feedback and approved policy change. This is a proposal; a conventional API is a valid alternative. No live server or measured reuse is claimed.
- Crop-health AI: offline capture and diagnosis after reconnection, with engineering/modelling/agronomy collaboration. Current GrowStac imagery remains a dated reference, not historical UI or diagnostic accuracy evidence.
- UrbanKhet: transition from terrace farming to direct produce supply; founder ownership spans customer offer, sourcing, fulfilment and economics. The original storefront shows the customer-facing ordering experience, not a completed checkout or audited delivery/profit.
- SilkRoute: retain the qualified programme GMV and a direct case link. No new causal interpretation.
